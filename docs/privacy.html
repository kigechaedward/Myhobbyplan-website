Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 26, 2026

Welcome to MyHobbyPlan

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how MyHobbyPlan (developed by Eleviq Technologies) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to transparency and compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.

1. Information We Collect

MyHobbyPlan is designed to respect your privacy by minimizing data collection. The app is fully usable without an account, and your planning data stays on your device unless you choose to sign in.

  • No Account Required: You do not need to provide a name, email, or phone number to use the app.
  • Local Data: All hobbies, schedules, and preferences you create are stored on your device. The weather-aware scoring and the smart notification engine both run entirely on-device.
  • Account Data (optional): If you sign in to enable cloud sync, we store your email address and account identifier along with the hobbies, settings and Pro status you choose to sync. We never sell this data.
  • Shared Mission Data: If you create or join a shared mission, the mission details, roster and messages you post are visible to the other members of that mission.
  • Device Information: We may collect non-sensitive device info (e.g., model, OS version) for performance monitoring.

2. Location Data & Weather Services

To provide weather-aware hobby planning, we process location data as follows:

  • Optional One-Tap GPS: The app declares the coarse and fine location permissions so you can fill in your city with a single tap during onboarding or in Settings. Your position is read once, at that moment, and is used only to resolve a nearby weather coordinate. It is never stored, never tracked in the background, and never shared beyond that lookup.
  • Manual Entry: You can skip GPS entirely and search for any city by name instead. Per-hobby locations are supported if a hobby happens somewhere other than your home city.
  • Third-Party Disclosure: The selected city is sent to OpenWeather to retrieve forecasts. No unique identifiers are shared with the weather provider.
  • Barometric Sensor: Where your device has a barometer, MyHobbyPlan reads local air pressure to detect incoming storms. Pressure readings stay on your device and are used only to generate alerts.

3. Advertising & Analytics (Third-Party Providers)

We use third-party services that may collect data used to identify you in accordance with their own policies:

  • Google AdMob: Used to serve advertisements. AdMob may collect your Advertising ID and device identifiers to show relevant ads. [View Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
  • Firebase / Google Analytics: Used to understand app performance and crashes, and to provide cloud sync and sign-in when you enable them. This data is anonymized and used only for app improvement.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Retention: Data stored locally remains on your device as long as the app is installed. If you sign in, your synced data is retained in our cloud backup until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Deletion: You can delete all your hobby data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the application. If you use cloud sync, deleting your account removes your synced data from our servers.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect the app, including authenticated cloud storage for synced data. Where your data is stored locally, the ultimate security of that data depends on your device's security settings (PIN, encryption, etc.).

6. Children's Privacy

MyHobbyPlan complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

7. Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page and effective immediately upon posting.
