Smart Forecasting

Your plan should
think ahead.

MyHobbyPlan scores every hobby against the forecast and your device's barometer, then nudges you toward the better window. The logic runs on your phone — no black box, and nothing leaves the device.

Available today

Live
  • Per-hobby weather scoring, tuned to each activity
  • Better-window alerts when a sharper 3-hour block beats your plan
  • Gear nudges built from temperature, wind, humidity and visibility
  • Storm and “conditions clearing” warnings from the on-device barometer
  • Plan-ahead prompts for days you have deliberately left empty
  • Four independent notification toggles: smart, alerts, reminders, planning

On the roadmap

In development
  • Voice-activated “mission start” planning
  • Leaderboard of the community's most reliable weather protocols
  • Full-screen 48-hour atmospheric trend charts
